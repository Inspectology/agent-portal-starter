# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, religion, sexual identity or orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, accept constructive feedback, focus on community benefit, protect private information, and assume good intent while addressing harmful impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, trolling, doxxing, publishing private correspondence, or conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct privately to the maintainers of the repository fork where it occurred. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, warn participants, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants. They will protect reporter privacy as far as practical and avoid conflicts of interest.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and offer an appeal to an uninvolved maintainer when one is available.
